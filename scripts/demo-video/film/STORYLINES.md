# Storylines

**Picked: A, "The thread".** Continuum's logo is literally the story (three dots on one thread: where work started, the handoff point, where it continues), so the abstract part, a handoff, becomes something a stranger can watch.

## A. The thread (picked)

- **Device:** one object across apps. The task travels from Claude Code, through Continuum, into Codex. **Form:** an object-to-object chain: each shot ends on the thing that opens the next.
- Claude Code fixes one bug and stops; its window collapses into a dot. Codex opens empty and you start re-explaining everything, then delete it. A thread draws from Claude's dot to Codex's, and the dot in the middle is Continuum. From there the real run: setup, the task card lifting out of Claude's terminal and carried into Codex as its briefing, the second fix, a verified test run, the real dashboard, and the logo performed as the thread it has been all along.
- **First two seconds:** Claude Code's terminal, large, already mid-run: its real reply streaming, ending "npm test still fails".
- **Beats:** hook (Claude stops) → tension (blank Codex, re-explaining) → reveal (the thread, the name) → connect → record → brief → finish → verify → dashboard → claim → end card.
- **Look:** the brand's warm paper with its dot grid, real dark terminals as the product surface, one iris accent for the thread, the next action and the end card; Geist and Geist Mono.
- **Sound:** a warm minimal electronic bed at 104 BPM that nearly drops out during the re-explaining and opens at the reveal; clicks, typing and whooshes only on visible actions; one riser and impact on the logo.

## B. The result first

- **Device:** the result first. **Form:** an endless zoom.
- Opens on the finished dashboard: one task, completed, verified, two agents. The camera dives into the Claude Code entry and lands in its terminal, then into the briefing, then into Codex, each detail opening the next world, and pulls back out to the dashboard.
- **First two seconds:** the dashboard's task header, "Fix pricing.js bugs · Completed", with both agents' marks.
- **Look and sound:** as A, with a steadier bed.
- *Not picked:* it shows the payoff before the viewer has felt the problem.

## C. Split screen to one

- **Device:** life before and after. **Form:** split screen to one.
- Left, a developer pasting the same explanation into agent after agent; right, Continuum's briefing arriving by itself. The panes collapse into the dashboard.
- *Not picked:* side-by-side panels are a tell, and it says the problem twice.
